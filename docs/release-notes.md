Stow hides the menu bar icons of the apps you choose behind one drawer icon. Click the drawer to show them, and they hide again on their own.

## Install

Download `Stow-__VERSION__.zip` below, unzip it, drag Stow to your Applications folder and open it. Stow needs macOS 27.

Stow relies on a private macOS framework. If an update breaks it, Stow leaves every icon visible.
